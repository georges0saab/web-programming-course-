ABOUT ME

I'm a Computer and Communications Engineering (CCE) student at Saint Joseph University of Beirut (USJ), currently part of the LebNet Tech Fellows program. I enjoy building things across different layers of tech, from web apps to embedded hardware.

My hands-on experience spans Python, JavaScript, C++, and HTML, with projects touching machine learning (PyTorch, logistic regression, Random Forest), embedded systems with Arduino and ESP32, and web development.

PROJECTS

1. LebNet final project — Smart Irrigation Advisor
AI-powered irrigation advisor extending my MiledsFarm ESP32 setup. Uses Random Forest or Logistic Regression to predict irrigation need from soil/weather data, benchmarked against MiledsFarm's rule-based logic.

2. MiledsFarm
Group Arduino/ESP32 smart farming project. My role is the decision logic layer — I built evaluateState() (priority-based rules mapping weather data to STORM/HOT/COLD/RAIN/WINDY/IDEAL), getAdviceSentence() for the OLED display, and NTP time sync for Lebanon (UTC+3).

3. Musing
AI-powered music practice and creation platform, built with Replit's AI agent on React/Node.js. Integrates Spotify, AudD, LRCLIB, Claude API, and YouTube Data API. Features song search, real-time chord/pitch detection, an instrument tuner (guitar, bass, ukulele, oud), AI lyric/chord generation, and karaoke video embedding. Submitted to the Kanz AI hackathon.
